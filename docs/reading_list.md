# Reading list

For the group (AI students, little finance background) and for the report. Every link was opened and checked on 16 Nov 2026. One line per item: title, source, and why it matters to this project.

## 1. Finance basics for beginners

- [Stocks](https://www.finra.org/investors/investing/investment-products/stocks), FINRA: what owning a share means; read the "Essentials" part first, it's the vocabulary for everything else here.
- [Stock market index](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stock_market_index), Wikipedia: what the S&P 500 and Nasdaq are and why they are cap-weighted, which is why a few Big Tech names can drag the whole index.
- [Exchange-Traded Funds and Products](https://www.finra.org/investors/investing/investment-products/exchange-traded-funds-and-products), FINRA: an ETF is a basket that tracks an index; this is why we can use SPY as "the market" and subtract its return in `RETURN_MODE = "excess"`.
- [Bull vs. bear market: What do they mean for you?](https://www.usbank.com/investing/financial-perspectives/investing-insights/bull-market-to-bear-market.html), U.S. Bank: the 20% rule for bull and bear markets; use it when we call 2022 a bear market in the report.
- [VIX](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/VIX), Wikipedia: the "fear index" measures expected S&P 500 volatility; high-VIX years are the years where many stocks cross their major-move threshold together.
- [What is Beta in Finance?](https://corporatefinanceinstitute.com/resources/valuation/what-is-beta-guide/), Corporate Finance Institute: beta and systematic (market) risk explain why most of our 40 stocks move with SPY, and why raw-return rules partly just rediscover the market.
- [Asset Allocation and Diversification](https://www.finra.org/investors/investing/investing-basics/asset-allocation-diversification), FINRA: diversification only works when assets are uncorrelated; when co-movement rises in a crisis, it stops working, which is the practical "so what" of our findings.
- [The Global Industry Classification Standard (GICS)](https://www.msci.com/indexes/index-resources/gics), MSCI: the official 11 sectors; use it to justify the four stock groups (Information Technology vs Communication Services vs the non-tech baseline).
- [Rising interest rates are hammering tech stocks](https://www.fullstackeconomics.com/p/rising-interest-rates-are-hammering-tech-stocks), Full Stack Economics (Timothy B. Lee, 2022): higher rates shrink the present value of far-off profits, so growth and tech stocks fall together when the Fed hikes (2022, June 2026).
- [Adjusted Closing Price](https://corporatefinanceinstitute.com/resources/equities/adjusted-closing-price/), Corporate Finance Institute: how splits and dividends are folded into past prices; `data/prices.csv` uses adjusted closes (`auto_adjust=True`), so a split (e.g. Nvidia's 10-for-1 in 2024) is not mistaken for a major move.

## 3. Market events behind our results, by year

- **2017** [Last Year, Not Last Week, Was Unusual for the Stock Market](https://www.callan.com/blog/volatility-2017/), Callan: 2017 had 53 days with the VIX below 10 and a record low of 8.56; expect few major moves and few rules that year.
- **2018** [Dow, S&P 500 post worst December since 1931, as Nasdaq has worst on record](https://www.foxbusiness.com/markets/dow-sp-500-having-worst-month-since-1931-as-grinch-hits-wall-st), Fox Business: the Q4 2018 selloff; explains clusters of joint DOWN days late in the year.
- **2020** [2020 stock market crash](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2020_stock_market_crash), Wikipedia: the COVID crash; the textbook case of "everything falls together", so expect many cross-group rules including the non-tech baseline.
- **2022** [It's been a vicious 6 months for stocks. Here's what the grim markets are signaling](https://www.ctpublic.org/2022-06-30/its-been-a-vicious-6-months-for-stocks-heres-what-the-grim-markets-are-signaling), NPR via Connecticut Public: worst first half in 50+ years, driven by Fed hikes; explains the all-red 2022 heatmap.
- **2022** [Fed attacks inflation with its largest rate hike since 1994](https://whyy.org/articles/fed-largest-rate-hike-since-1994/), AP via WHYY: the 0.75-point hike of 15 June 2022; a single policy shock that moves the whole market on one day.
- **2023-24** [What's going on with Nvidia stock and the booming AI market?](https://www.techtarget.com/whatis/feature/Whats-going-on-with-Nvidia-stock-and-the-booming-AI-market), TechTarget: Nvidia's run from $1T (mid-2023) to $3T (June 2024); the AI-enabler rally after ChatGPT, where AI stocks moved UP together while others didn't.
- **2025 Jan** [China's DeepSeek causes rout among AI-linked stocks, Nvidia shares tumble](https://www.aljazeera.com/economy/2025/1/27/chinas-deepseek-causes-rout-among-ai-linked-stocks), Al Jazeera: DeepSeek's cheap model hit AI-linked stocks on 27 Jan 2025; an AI-specific shock, not a market-wide one.
- **2025 Jan** [China's DeepSeek derails Nasdaq, Nvidia, AI darlings](https://www.foxbusiness.com/markets/chinas-deepseek-derails-nasdaq-nvidia-ai-darlings), Fox Business: Nvidia down almost 17% and about $600B in market value lost in one day; a clear example of AI enablers making joint DOWN moves.
- **2025 Apr** [Dow tumbles more than 1,600 the day after Trump's major tariff announcement](https://www.pbs.org/newshour/economy/u-s-stock-market-tumbles-the-day-after-trumps-major-tariff-announcement), PBS News: the "Liberation Day" crash of 3 Apr 2025, the S&P 500's worst day since 2020; a market-wide shock that the excess-return setting should filter out.
- **2025 Apr** [10 days and a $10 trillion market swing](https://fortune.com/2025/04/12/trump-tariffs-stocks-bonds-supply-chain-what-comes-next/), Fortune: the crash and the rebound after the tariff pause; big joint UP days follow the joint DOWN days.
- **2026 Feb** [A stock market doom loop is hitting everything that touches AI](https://fortune.com/2026/02/15/ai-stock-market-selloff-doom-loop-real-estate-services-wealth-management-insurance-logistics/), Fortune (Bloomberg): investors sold companies seen as threatened by AI, software included; relevant to the traditional-software group.
- **2026 Jun** [Tech stocks lead market bloodbath as fears of Fed rate hikes add to worries about the AI-fueled chip boom petering out](https://www.fortune.com/2026/06/05/tech-stocks-market-bloodbath-fed-rate-hikes-jobs-report-ai-fueled-chip-boom/), Fortune: chip stocks plus rate-hike fears on 5 Jun 2026; AI enablers and Big Tech falling together.
- **2026 Jun** [Global Tech Selloff Underway, Hitting Broader Market as Well](https://www.cboe.com/insights/posts/global-tech-selloff-underway-hitting-broader-market-as-well), Cboe: the June 2026 tech selloff spreading to the wider market; useful for "did tech moves pull the baseline along?".
- **2026 Oct** [Tech stocks wipe out $1 trillion in value as concerns deepen about the AI boom](https://www.nbcnews.com/business/markets/tech-stocks-nasdaq-ai-boom-fears-deepen-rcna606512), NBC News: the 30 Oct 2026 selloff over AI-spending doubts (Nasdaq 100 down 3.4%); the most recent shock in our 2026 data.

## 4. Academic papers for the Related Work section

- [Mining association rules between sets of items in large databases](https://doi.org/10.1145/170035.170072), Agrawal, Imielinski and Swami, ACM SIGMOD 1993: the original association rules paper (support, confidence, the market-basket idea we reuse with trading days as baskets).
- [Mining frequent patterns without candidate generation](https://doi.org/10.1145/342009.335372), Han, Pei and Yin, ACM SIGMOD 2000: the FP-Growth algorithm we mine each year with (section 4).
- [Hierarchical structure in financial markets](https://doi.org/10.1007/s100510050929), Mantegna, European Physical Journal B 11, 1999 ([free arXiv copy](https://arxiv.org/abs/cond-mat/9802256)): clusters stocks by return correlation into a tree; the closest precedent for our section 8 clustering cross-check.
- [No Contagion, Only Interdependence: Measuring Stock Market Comovements](https://doi.org/10.1111/0022-1082.00494), Forbes and Rigobon, Journal of Finance 57, 2002 ([free NBER working paper](https://www.nber.org/papers/w7267)): co-movement looks higher in crises partly because volatility is higher; a warning to keep in mind before reading 2020 and 2022 as "contagion".
- [Data Mining and Machine Learning: Fundamental Concepts and Algorithms (2nd ed.)](https://dataminingbook.info/), Zaki and Meira, Cambridge University Press 2020 (DOI [10.1017/9781108564175](https://doi.org/10.1017/9781108564175)): our course textbook, free to read online; cite it for itemset mining, rule measures and clustering.
- [Determination of the world stock indices' co-movements by association rule mining](https://doi.org/10.1108/JEFAS-04-2020-0150), Kartal, Sert and Kutlu, Journal of Economics, Finance and Administrative Science 27(54), 2022, open access: Apriori on up/down days of 11 world indices over 2001-2019 as one pooled dataset; we use individual stocks, keep only major moves, and mine each year separately to see change over time.
- [A novel discovery model for revealing substitution relationships from international stock markets: With association rule analysis](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.heliyon.2024.e38774), Dai, Huang, Yu and Gu, Heliyon 10(20), 2024, open access: compares Apriori, FP-Growth and Eclat on rise/fall days of foreign indices and Taiwan's top 50 stocks (2014-2018), using next-day lags; we look at same-day moves of US stocks only and add a shuffle test against chance.
- [Stock market prediction using weighted inter-transaction class association rule mining and evolutionary algorithm](https://doi.org/10.1080/1331677X.2022.2043762), Chen, Mo and Zhang, Economic Research-Ekonomska Istraživanja 35(1), 2022, open access: uses rules across days to predict stock direction; their goal is prediction, ours is describing how patterns change, which is the data mining framing the course asks for.
